# Trusted Spots 1-Minute Trading Strategy Guide

This guide synthesizes the trading strategy and lessons from the "TRUSTED SPOTS" 100-Day/100K Trading Challenge.

## 1. Strategy Overview
- **Platform**: Pocket Option (or Quotex).
- **Timeframe**: 1-Minute Candlesticks.
- **Expiry**: 1 Minute.
- **Style**: Pure Price Action Reversals (No Indicators).

## 2. Identifying "Trusted Spots" (Strong SNR)
A "Trusted Spot" is a high-probability Support or Resistance level. A level must meet as many of these 5 criteria as possible:
1. **Extreme Levels**: The absolute highest and lowest points in the recent chart view.
2. **Series of Rejections**: Multiple touches and bounces off the level (best in ranging markets).
3. **Obviousness**: The level should be easy to spot within 3 seconds.
4. **Drastic Movement**: Price must move away sharply and significantly after touching the level.
5. **Role Reversal (S/R Flip)**: The level has acted as both support and resistance in the past.

## 3. Multi-Timeframe Confirmation Layer
A key lesson from the challenge is using multiple timeframes to increase accuracy:
- **1-Minute Chart**: Used for overall market structure and identifying "Trusted Spots" (SNR levels).
- **5-Second Chart**: Used for micro-confirmation. Dropping down to the 5-second timeframe allows you to see the "rejection" in real-time.
- **The Confirmation**: When price hits a 1-minute level, switch to the 5-second chart. Look for the price to "struggle" at the level and then produce a clear rejection candle or a quick reversal move away from the spot.

## 4. Execution Workflow
1. **Analysis**: Identify and draw "Trusted Spots" on the 1-minute chart using the 5 criteria.
2. **Watch**: Wait for the price to approach and touch the 1-minute level.
3. **Confirm**: Switch to the **5-second timeframe**.
4. **Observe Rejection**: Look for a clear rejection sign (e.g., 5-second reversal candle or a quick bounce) that proves the level is holding.
5. **Entry**: Immediately enter a 1-minute reversal trade (Call/Higher at support, Put/Lower at resistance).

## 5. Trading Challenge Principles & Psychology
- **Selective Trading**: Take only the best setups. Quality > Quantity.
- **Risk Management**: Never increase stake size after a loss (No Martingale).
- **Avoid Revenge Trading**: Accept losses as part of the business; do not try to "win back" money immediately.
- **Avoid FOMO**: The market always provides more opportunities. Wait for your setup.
- **Gambler's Fallacy**: Each trade is an independent event. Past results do not change the odds of the next trade.
